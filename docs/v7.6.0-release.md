# ManagerStory V7.6.0

Release: 2026-10-16, by user decision after reviewing the iPhone preview of `dev/v7.6.0` ("V7.6 yı canlıya alalım"). Details and measurements per change are in `docs/v7.6.0-progress.md`.

## What is new

Career
- The day's decision sits in a tray above the bottom navigation instead of far down the page; it collapses to one line.

Match engine
- Shooting and passing decisions follow a realistic shot-value curve: fewer hopeful long shots, more shots from the box.
- Passes into a runner's path; offside is decided by real positions (attackers hold the line, a flag depends on how far beyond they are), no more random calls.
- Corners come from defenders as in real football: cut-out crosses and box balls, lost aerial duels, challenges won on the byline, deflected misses (2.1 → about 4 a match).
- The referee lets a promising attack finish before the final whistle.
- The team block moves up and down the pitch with the ball and stays more compact; passers prefer nearer team-mates (mean pass 31 → 25 m).

New 3D view
- Recorded CC0 motions: sliding tackles, header jumps on corners (target and marker both go up), the scorer's celebration.
- Players walk, jog, run and sprint by situation instead of standing and then all jogging; sprints for runs in behind, pressing from distance and chasing long balls; dribbling speeds up in space and slows down near a man.
- A more compact jogging stride (the recorded jog opened wider than a sprint).
- A free-kick or offside whistle stops play where it is with a banner; at full time everyone eases to a stop and the last actions still play.
- Lofted balls allow for air drag, so crosses arrive where they are aimed.

Version labels, script cache URLs, manifest and offline cache key are 7.6.0. Career saves keep their data (version stamp only).

## Known limits

Passes and shots still use hand-keyed actions (the free motion library has no football kick). The engine plays one or two actions a minute, so one engine pass often stands for a short sequence (real mean pass 17–20 m). About 4 corners and 10 crosses a match against 10 and 20+ in real football. Running and sprinting are still rarer than in real football.
