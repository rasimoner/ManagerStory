# ManagerStory V7.8.1 — development

Branch `dev/v7.8.1`, started 2026-10-17 from `main` (V7.8.0), after the user's review of the live V7.8.0 on the iPhone (three recordings, 17 Oct 22:25, 22:44, 22:48).

## Free kick: the wall and the team-mate on the ball

The user: the wall is not set up; one of our players stands on the ball.

What the recordings and three matches showed: the engine puts 3–4 defenders on the wall line 9.15 m out, 0.62 m apart, but then spaces every player at least ~3 m from his team-mates (the same spacing the 2D pitch uses), so the "wall" stood spread over 8 m with gaps (measured across the line to goal: −4.1, −1.6, +1.6, +3.7 m). The player who was fouled was left where the foul happened, about 2 m in front of the ball, right in the line of the shot.

3D view: at a direct free kick the defenders the engine put in the wall line up shoulder to shoulder (0.7 m apart, 9.4 m out, shifted half a metre to the near post's side; the keeper covers the other half), and the taker's team-mates within 6 m of the ball step 5 m to the side of the line to goal. The kick waits until they are in place. Three matches: wall across −1.7…+0.6 m, nearest team-mate 4.6 m to the side.

## Offside: what the picture showed

The recording: our pass was intercepted, the opponent carried the ball forward and "OFSAYT" came for his forward. The call itself was the engine's (long ball from the carrier to a forward beyond our second-last defender). What the picture could not explain:
- the yellow line was drawn where the engine's second-last defender stood, while the 3D defenders stood elsewhere (in 15 calls over 6 matches, 3–6 m apart typically, once 40 m);
- the receiver was where the 3D view had him, sometimes 20–40 m behind the engine's spot, so at the moment of the pass he was not beyond any line on screen (2 of 15 calls: 2 and 22 m behind it).

3D view now:
- the line shown is the second-last defender on the pitch as it is (keeper included), never inside the attackers' own half;
- the receiver starts his run a few events before the call;
- the passer holds the ball until the receiver is beyond that line (up to 3.5 s), the pass goes beyond the line, the flag goes up as it is played.
Six matches: the receiver is beyond the drawn line at the pass in every call (0.7–26 m).

## Through on goal, passing back

The recording: a through ball put our forward in on goal, the keeper came out and missed it, and he turned and passed back towards midfield (intercepted).

Engine: after a through ball (34 matches, 479 received), the receiver passed in 95 cases, 21 of them backwards; he shot in 172. The pass decision only discounted backward passes when he was in a good shooting position, and a pass kept at least a third of its weight.

Now: a player through on goal — inside the last 30 m, no outfield defender between him and the goal — goes for goal: the pass and hold-up options are cut to a fraction, shooting is strongly preferred (the chance step can still square it to a better-placed team-mate). 34 matches: after a through ball, passes 95 → 73 (backwards 21 → 12), shots 172 → 177, carries towards goal 85 → 110. Goals per match 3.24 → 3.56, shots 21.0 → 23.8 (the same 34 fixture seeds).
