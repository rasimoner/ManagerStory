# ManagerStory V7.7.0

Release: 2026-10-16, by user decision after reviewing the iPhone preview of `dev/v7.7.0` ("bu halini koru, versiyona ver"). Details and measurements are in `docs/v7.7.0-progress.md`.

## What is new

New 3D view
- The ball is no longer put at a player's feet: receivers go after long clearances and passes, a pass that rolls past its spot is chased, kickers run up to the ball instead of lunging at it, a save meets the keeper's gloves, headers and sliding tackles play the ball only on a real touch.
- Passes reach their man at a receivable pace (long ground passes used to stop short).
- Restarts: the taker runs over and steps onto the spot; throw-ins and keeper catches take the ball into the hands.
- A long dribble never freezes the match; sent-off and substituted players leave when the event is played.
- The camera is the V7.6 camera. (An edge-of-frame correction tried during development made the picture stutter on the iPhone and was rolled back.)

Clean-up
- The old 3D renderer is removed (`?eski3d=1`, `?dev3d=1`, its modules and its 50+ presentation tests). The new view is the only 3D view; if it cannot load, the match falls back to 2D.

Tests
- `tests/newview-director.test.cjs`: complete matches through the real view (no stalls, no teleports, ball in play unless the engine says so, no snaps, real contacts, smooth camera).
- `tests/match-clock.test.cjs`: the shared presentation clock and the 2D pitch on complete matches.
- The whole suite is green again, so the standard publish workflow publishes on its own; the reviewed-publish workflows (V7.4.3, V7.5.0, V7.6.0), which pointed at removed tests, are removed.

Version labels, script cache URLs, manifest and offline cache key are 7.7.0. Career saves keep their data (version stamp only).

## Checks

- `npm test`: 142/142.
- New view over 34 matches: every event played, no stall, no snap, no teleport.
- Browser: full career matches in fullscreen, every engine event played, same result as the engine, 0 page errors.

## Known limits

Matches take longer to play out on the pitch than in V7.6 (about 13.9 against 12.1 minutes at 1x), because players run to the ball and passes travel instead of being placed. In the narrow portrait frame the ball can leave the picture on a fast pass (as in V7.6). Some engine events (an offside or a free kick) can appear without a visible cause on the pitch.
