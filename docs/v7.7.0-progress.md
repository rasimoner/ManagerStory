# ManagerStory V7.7.0 — development

Branch `dev/v7.7.0`, started 2026-10-16 from `main` (V7.6.0).

## Old 3D renderer removed

The new view has been the only 3D view in the career since V7.5. The old renderer was still shipped and reachable with `?eski3d=1` (and `?dev3d=1`), and its 50+ presentation tests were the reason the full suite was red and publication needed a reviewed workflow.

Removed: `match3d-career.js`, `match3d-scene.js`, `match3d-player.js`, `match3d-keeper.js`, `match3d-net.js`, `match3d-football-pose.js`, `match3d-live-view.js`, `match3d-pass-*` (timeline, replay, recordings), `match3d-dev.js/.css`, every `tests/match3d-*.test.cjs`, the `tools/match3d-*.mjs` scripts, `tools/record-3d-pass-examples.cjs` and `tools/fixtures/`. `index.html` loads only the new view (falls back to 2D if it cannot load); the offline cache no longer lists the removed files.

Kept: `match3d-live-clock.js` (the shared presentation clock, also drives the 2D pitch), `match-view-adapter.js`, `match3d-career.css` (used by the new view).

## New tests for the new view

`tests/newview-director.test.cjs` plays complete engine matches through the real director and runtime (THREE in node, no page), at 120 steps a second, and checks for any fixture (`NV_FIXTURES`, default `3,7`):

- every engine event is played in order, nothing stalls, the score shown is the engine's;
- nobody teleports: drawn body speed under 10.5 m/s (keeper dives 13 m/s), no body jump over 0.3 m or ball jump over 0.6 m in one frame outside placed restarts;
- the ball leaves the pitch only when the engine says so;
- possession never changes by putting the ball at a player's feet ("snap");
- the ball is played by what touches it: foot, sliding leg, forehead, keeper's gloves within 0.45 m.

`tests/match-clock.test.cjs` checks the shared clock on complete matches (`CLOCK_FIXTURES`, default `0,7`): the match through the clock is the engine's (same events, score, statistics), one engine minute at a time, half and full time once, the result applied once, each goal graphic waits for the team photo and a failed photo does not stop the match, pause freezes everything, speed changes never restart an event, the 2D pitch never jumps, score and statistics never run ahead, switching 2D/3D or tabs keeps the match.

Camera framing is measured in the browser (`ManagerStoryNewView.framing`: where the ball is on screen, sampled 5 times a second during play), not in node.

## Camera keeps the ball in frame

The framing check found the ball off screen in 15% of samples at 2x in portrait: the camera eased after the ball in real time, so at 2x a long pass ran out of the narrow portrait frame. The camera now follows in match time and, if the ball would still be outside the middle of the frame, closes in on its planned position; when a close-up or shot camera is planned around a player while the ball has gone elsewhere, it turns to the ball.

## Ball teleports fixed (new view)

What the tests found and what changed (`dist/newview/director.js`, `runtime.js`):

- Long clearances and passes: the receiver gave up after a fixed time and the ball was put at his feet. Now he keeps going after the ball while he is closing in; he goes after the ball itself once it has rolled past the spot it was played to.
- Kicks: the kicker lunged at the ball (up to 50 m/s) when the kick started too far away. He now runs up to the ball first; the kick only covers a running step.
- Restarts: the taker walks to the ball instead of being placed; throw-ins and keeper catches take the ball into the hands with a short blend.
- Saves: the keeper sets himself on the line between ball and goal, plans the dive first and the shot is aimed at where the gloves will be. Before, the ball sometimes flew behind the line and was put back into his hands.
- Headers and sliding tackles change the ball only on a real touch (forehead / leg within 0.45 m); the jumper sets off when the cross is hit, within his reach.
- A kick re-aimed after the run-up (the ball rolled on, or the pass was to a spot right next to it) is approached again instead of lunging at it.
- A save when the shooter is already level with or past the keeper is smothered on its way to the line; a shot on its way to the gloves is not bumped off another player's body.
- A long run with the ball slows down only while an opponent is near, and ends after about 11 s wherever the carrier is (it used to run past the 14 s safety stop).
- Players bumping into each other separate gently (≤ 1.2 cm a step) instead of being pushed apart.
- The ball is never snapped to the ground or back into the pitch; the sent-off or substituted player leaves when the event is played, not earlier.
