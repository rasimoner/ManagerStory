# ManagerStory V7.9.0 — development

Branch `dev/v7.9.0`, started 2026-10-18 from `main` (V7.8.2). Aim: the "star moment" — a gifted player's skill shown so that it can be read, in the real match.

Background: two prototype videos made in this session (hand-keyed step-over, nutmeg, elastico, chest control, no-look pass on the game's own runtime) and two made by ChatGPT for the user. The second ChatGPT prototype placed the boots with a two-bone foot solver (hip–knee–ankle) instead of keyed angles: the boot really goes round the ball and the standing foot stays on the grass. That is the technique taken here, together with its idea of measurable move checks ("the move must be recognisable with its name hidden").

## Step 1 — foot targets, step-over and nutmeg in real matches

- Runtime: `footIK` turns the recorded skeleton's hip–knee–ankle chain so a toe reaches a given point (knee bending forwards, boot flat along the heading); a skill move sets `p.feet = { Left|Right: { at(t), w(t) } }` and the pose applies it every frame.
- A duel the attacker wins can now be a step-over or a nutmeg (the engine decides the duel; the move is chosen per event, so a match always looks the same):
  - Step-over: he settles the ball in front of his boots and turns square to it, the left boot is planted beside it, the right boot circles the front of the ball from inside to out (lifted to ~0.3 m) without touching it; the defender buys it and goes that way; the same boot comes back to the ball's side and takes it across the other way; the defender stumbles. Slow motion and the close camera during the move.
  - Nutmeg: the defender squares up close in front with his feet planted apart, a short stab with the right boot sends the ball through the gap, the attacker goes round him and collects; the defender turns to look.
  - During a move the ball is played only by the boots (no body nudges).
- Checks (`tests/newview-director.test.cjs`, every skill move in every match): the step-over boot stays at least 12 cm off the ball and lifts over 12 cm, the standing boot slips under 5 cm, the take and the stab are made with the boot within 30 cm of the ball; the nutmeg ball goes between the defender's feet with at least 15 cm to each. Measured: step-over closest approach 19–20 cm, lift 27–31 cm, standing foot still; nutmeg 42–45 cm to each foot.
- Highlight renderer (development tool, not in the game): plays a recorded engine match through the game's director and runtime in the browser, finds the skill moves and records those moments with a star camera; used for the preview video.

## Step 2 — special moves for special players, only where they fit; three more moves; the star replay

The user: "bazı çalımları her oyuncu yapmasın özel olsun — bazı bölgelerde de bu çalımlardan bazıları yapılmasın".

### Who has which move (`pitch-v731.js` `skillMoves`)

A player's repertoire comes from his own attributes (dribbling, technique, vision) and his role; keepers have none.

| Move | Who has it |
|---|---|
| Nutmeg | 4–5 star dribblers (flair = dribbling·0.6 + technique·0.4 ≥ 77), or 3 stars with technique ≥ 76 |
| Step-over | 4–5 star dribblers, not centre-backs |
| Elastico | 5 stars, or 4 stars with dribbling and technique both ≥ 80; not centre-backs |
| No-look pass | vision ≥ 78 and technique ≥ 74 |

In our squad: Aras (CAM) has nutmeg, step-over and the elastico; Kerem (RW) and Okoro (LW) have nutmeg and step-over; nobody else has a special move. Over 12 fixtures (both teams, 240 outfield players) about three in ten have one, the elastico is very rare, and the no-look pass belongs to a few playmakers.

The view gets the repertoire with the roster (`moves`), and only a player who has a move does it.

### Where (director `zoneOk`)

Measured from the player's own goal line:
- in his own third (inside 35 m, his own box included): no step-over, nutmeg or elastico;
- the elastico and the no-look pass only in the opponents' half; the elastico not within 3 m of the touchline.

A beaten defender falls for a special move about two times in three when the carrier has one that fits there; otherwise the duel is one of the usual ones (feint and cut, knock past, drag-back).

### New moves (boots placed with the foot targets)

- Elastico: the outside of the right boot pushes the ball out to his right, the same boot wraps round the front of the ball and the inside snaps it back to his left; the left boot stays planted; the defender goes with the first touch and stumbles.
- No-look pass: a pass played forwards or square in the opponents' half by a player who has it, struck while his head looks the other way (the `nolook` motion), in slow motion.
- Chest control: some long balls dropping onto a team-mate who is already under them are taken on the chest and fall to his feet (any outfield player; if he is not under it in time he plays it as it comes).

### Star replay (page)

The page keeps the last ~3.5 s of the pitch (every body and the ball). When a special move happens (step-over, nutmeg, elastico, no-look pass) the play goes on for 0.9 s, then the match waits and the moment is shown again from a high close camera at 0.35× speed, with "TEKRAR · YILDIZ KAMERASI", the speed, a progress bar, the move's name, the player and a one-line explanation; "Geç ›" skips it. At most one replay every 30 s of play. The replay speed is a row in the speed panel (0 turns replays off).

### Checks (step 2)

- `tests/newview-director.test.cjs`, every special move in every match: elastico — both touches with the boot within 30 cm of the ball, the ball goes out at least 12 cm before it comes back, the standing boot slips under 6 cm; no-look pass — struck with the boot within 35 cm of the ball; chest control — the ball within 38 cm of the chest, above 0.9 m. And for every move but chest control: the player has it, it is not in his own third, the elastico and the no-look pass only in the opponents' half, no elastico against the touchline.
- First full run: 230/238 — chest balls passing 40–50 cm from the chest (he stood on the landing spot instead of putting his chest in the ball's path) and two no-look passes struck 0.5–0.6 m from a ball still bouncing. Fixed: the chest goes to where the ball arrives; a no-look pass on a bouncing ball, or when he cannot get set, becomes an ordinary pass. Measured after: chest 11–33 cm, no-look 6–17 cm, elastico 12–15 cm with the ball 35 cm out.
- New view over 34 matches: 238/238. `npm test`: 146/146.

### Development tools (not visible in the game)

- `ManagerStoryNewView.star('stepover' | 'nutmeg' | 'elastico' | 'nolook')`: the match waits while a player who has that move is put in the opponents' half with the ball and a defender comes to meet him; the move and its replay then play exactly as in a match. Used to film the preview video.
- `?nvfixdt&nvmanual`: every frame is 1/60 s and a recording script steps the frames itself (`ManagerStoryNewView.step(n)`), so a slow machine still records a smooth video.
- Temporary "Yıldız anı dene" buttons in the speed panel (Makas, Bacak arası, Elastico, Bakmadan pas): the match does not stop; the next time one of our outfield players has the ball with an opponent within 16 m, that opponent comes to him and he plays the move (for the test, whether he has it or not and wherever he is), its star replay follows and the match goes on from there. The no-look pass is played on our next short pass. To be removed before release.

## Step 3 — more special moves (batch 1)

The user sent two videos (an FC 26 "special skills per star level" clip with the flair nutmeg, and Neymar's flicks against Rennes) and asked for more variety. Our own versions, made with the same foot targets; nothing copied from either.

| Move | Who has it | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Cruyff turn: shapes to strike it, the boot comes through beside the ball, the inside drags it back behind the standing leg and he turns away | ★3+ and technique 70+ | not in his own third |
| La Croqueta: right boot slides it across to the left boot, which takes it on past the defender | ★4 and a midfielder, or ★4 with technique 80+ | not in his own third |
| Marseille turn: a full turn on the ball, right sole then left sole, out past his man | ★5, or ★4 with technique 80+; not centre-backs | not in his own third |
| Flair nutmeg: turns his back on the defender, the heel sends it back through the defender's legs, he spins round him | has the nutmeg, ★4, dribbling 78+ | opponents' half |
| Step-over pass: a real step-over, and the pass is played in the middle of it while the defender goes with the move | has the step-over, vision 75+ | opponents' half, a defender right in front, a short pass on |

In our squad Aras now has eight moves (nutmeg, step-over, elastico, Cruyff, croqueta, Marseille, flair nutmeg, step-over pass), Kerem and Okoro four (nutmeg, step-over, Cruyff, flair nutmeg), Burak and Taylan the Cruyff turn.

Checks per move (every match): Cruyff — boot lifted back over 18 cm, through at least 10 cm from the ball, the take within 30 cm, the ball passes behind the standing boot, which stays put; croqueta — one touch with each boot within 30 cm, the ball moves across over 20 cm; Marseille — a full turn (over 5.5 rad), each sole within 30 cm of the ball, the ball within 90 cm of him through the turn; flair nutmeg — half a turn first, the heel within 30 cm, the ball between the defender's feet with 15 cm to each; step-over pass — the step-over checks and the pass with the boot on the ball.

## Step 3 — batch 2 (showboating) and a clean-up pass

The user found faults on the phone and asked to go back to slower, cleaner deliveries. Every move was played on the real page frame by frame (the move live, its replay, the play after it) and the faults found were fixed:
- after a move played from the test button the interrupted engine event was resumed and put the ball back where the engine had it (a jump): the next event now starts from the pitch as it is;
- in the Cruyff and Marseille turns the defender ran into the attacker; he now goes for the faked shot about a metre off, or the other side;
- after a move the two-man close-up stayed on as the attacker ran away and lost him; the camera now goes back to the wide picture as he gets away, and as soon as the ball is lifted in the showboat moves and the step-over pass;
- a player standing right in front of a close camera filled the screen; he is left out of the picture (never the players the shot is about);
- one player repeated the same move three times in a match; a move he has shown waits until his others have had their turn.

Showboat moves (opponents' half only, never when his team is losing):

| Move | Who has it |
|---|---|
| Rainbow flick: rolled back under the sole, popped up behind the calf, flicked over his head and the defender's by the heel | ★5, or ★4 with dribbling 82+; not centre-backs |
| Sombrero: face to face, the toe scoops it over the defender's head, round him and collect | ★5, or ★4 with dribbling and technique 81+ |
| Keep it up and go: toe flick up, a second touch in the air over the lunging leg | ★5 |

How often: a beaten defender falls for a special move 30% of the time against a ★3 dribbler, 60% against ★4, 75% against ★5; among the moves a player has, the showier ones are weighted up. 34 matches: Cruyff 36, nutmeg 24, no-look pass 11, step-over 9, croqueta 5, Marseille 2, step-over pass 2, elastico 1 (plus 63 chest controls).

Checks: rainbow — sole and heel within 30 cm, the ball over 1.9 m and over the defender above 1.7 m; sombrero — toe within 30 cm, over 1.8 m and over the defender above 1.7 m; keep it up — two touches within 30 cm, the ball off the grass between them, over the lunging leg. New view over 34 matches: 238/238.
