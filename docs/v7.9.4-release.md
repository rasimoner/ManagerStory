# ManagerStory V7.9.4

Release: 2026-10-20, by user decision ("bitince main e birleştir"). Takes in V7.9.3 (keeper part 3 and the fixes from the user's phone, never released on its own) and the open-play crosses. Details in `docs/v7.9.0-progress.md` ("Keeper, part 3", "Fixes from the user's phone (V7.9.3 preview)", "Open-play crosses (V7.9.4)").

## What is new

- Keeper, part 3: punches (engine: on corners and contested crosses into the box), one on one (comes off his line, never out of his box; saves at the man's feet), penalties (on his line, goes as it is struck; "PENALTI KURTARIŞI" replay), the volley out of his hands, rolling over and getting up after the scorpion.
- From the user's phone: a caught ball stays in the keeper's hands; no grey screen (no camera shot inside a stand); a save turned behind goes over the bar or round the post (no flight to the corner flag); an attacker in space no longer plays a long ball back to his own half; very long balls fly flatter.
- Open-play crosses (engine): a wide man in the attacking third with team-mates in the box crosses into a zone (near post, penalty spot, far post) that one of them attacks; contested in the air like a corner (header, knock-down, clearance or corner, the keeper claims or punches). Wings and width tactics cross more. On the pitch the crosser crosses from out wide and the runner meets it.
- Temporary "Kaleci sahnesi (geçici)" buttons in the speed panel (with the other test buttons, to be removed when the user says).

Version labels, script cache URLs, manifest and offline cache key are 7.9.4. Career saves keep their data.
