# ManagerStory V7.8.1

Release: 2026-10-18, by user decision after the V7.8.1 preview ("Evet al"). Details and measurements are in `docs/v7.8.1-progress.md`.

## What is new

- Direct free kicks: the wall stands shoulder to shoulder 9.15 m out and covers the near post; the taker's team-mates step out of the line to goal; the kick waits until they are in place (3D view).
- Offside: the line shown is the second-last defender as he stands on the pitch; the receiver starts his run early and the pass is played once he is beyond that line, so the call can be seen (3D view).
- A player through on goal (inside the last 30 m, no outfield defender between him and the goal) goes for goal instead of turning back (engine). Goals per match on the fixed test seeds 3.24 → 3.56.
- The passing-style test now measures over 16 matches per style (8 sat at the edge of its threshold).

Version labels, script cache URLs, manifest and offline cache key are 7.8.1. Career saves keep their data.
