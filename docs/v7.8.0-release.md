# ManagerStory V7.8.0

Release: 2026-10-17, by user decision after reviewing the iPhone preview of `dev/v7.8.0` ("bu hale kadar olan geliştirmeleri main e birleştir ve yayınla"). Details and measurements are in `docs/v7.8.0-progress.md`.

## What is new

Matches
- Every match is a new match: a career's matches are no longer seeded by the fixture number alone (every new career used to play the same first match, event for event). A match in progress still saves and restores its dice.
- Fouls are real challenges: committed by the side without the ball, by the man nearest the ball, where the ball is; free kick, advantage in the final third, or a penalty in the box. Cards follow the foul (from behind, stopping an attack, persistent fouling, last man).
- Direct free kicks within shooting range: a wall of 3–4 at 9.15 m, the specialist takes it, close-up camera behind the taker, the ball bent over the wall.
- Passing: fewer back passes with open grass ahead; a forward no longer plays long balls back to his own defence.
- Offside: the pass is played to the man beyond the line, the flag goes up, the offside line is shown.
- Commentary: varied sentences for passes, touches, carries, duels and restarts.

3D view
- Keeper: a set position that moves (bouncing, weight shifting); he takes up his position between ball and goal when the ball is near and races back before a save; no more saves by a keeper standing behind the shooter.
- Net catches the ball and ripples; header jumps at a real height.
- Restarts: players take up the engine's arrangement (full box at corners, no opponents in the box at goal kicks); opponents keep 9.15 m at free kicks and corners; penalty and free-kick takers face the goal.
- Team shape: the side without the ball squeezes towards it; a ball played into space is contested.
- Duels: shoulder to shoulder, stepping in, knock-on and race, drag-back, besides the poke and the feint.
- A shot that misses goes just wide, not towards the corner flag.
- Attack direction chip and radar arrow.
- Subtitles appear when the action happens (the kick, or the end of a duel), not when the player starts running.
- Camera: cuts to a far new shot instead of swinging; the stand camera stays on the TV side, so left and right never swap; the close-up keeps its side.
- Temporary speed panel ("⚙ Hız"): what the 0.5×/1×/2× buttons really play at, players' pace, ball pace, camera easing, shot slow motion. Saved on the device only; the defaults are the previous game. It stays until the user has chosen his values.

Version labels, script cache URLs, manifest and offline cache key are 7.8.0. Career saves keep their data (a match salt is added on load).
